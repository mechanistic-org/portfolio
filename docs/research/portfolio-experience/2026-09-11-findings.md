## Evidence method and limits

The labels below distinguish three kinds of statement:

- **Observed:** directly inspected page text, accessibility tree, screenshot or
  interaction in the desktop embedded browser during this research.
- **Documented:** behavior or intent described by the creator or the relevant
  standards body. Documentation does not establish that the behavior worked in
  this browser session.
- **Inference / transfer choice:** a proposed application to this portfolio. It
  is a design judgment to test, not an empirical result supplied by the reference.

This was a bounded desktop observation, not a comparative usability study,
performance benchmark, full accessibility audit, or mobile validation. No
attention-span, conversion, retention, engagement-duration or causal performance
claim follows from it. External sites can change after the access date.

## First-party source and behavior matrix

### Machinarium / Amanita Design

**Observed:** The official product page exposes conventional navigation, an
embedded trailer, platform and demo links, and a screenshot gallery. Its current
free-demo links lead to Steam and Google Play; no browser-playable demo was
verified. [Amanita product page](https://amanita-design.net/games/machinarium.html)

**Observed:** The inspected official still places Josef, stairs, railway, doors
and machinery in one connected, stationary composition. Distinct objects remain
available for inspection within the same frame. This is evidence about the
composition of that still, not evidence of interactive affordances or game
navigation. [Inspected official scene](https://amanita-design.net/img/games/machinarium-01@2x.jpg)

**Documented:** Amanita describes collecting items and solving environmental
puzzles. Its developer-authored storefront description identifies hand-drawn
backgrounds and characters, and symbolic animated comic bubbles for
communication. [Amanita product page](https://amanita-design.net/games/machinarium.html),
[Amanita's Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/40700/Machinarium/)

**Inference / transfer choice:** Borrow connected composition, recognizable
landmarks and contextual detail. A stable frame can contain a rich environment;
continuous camera movement is not the only way to make it interesting. Portfolio
essentials should remain explicit text and links. Inventory puzzles, hidden
hotspots and symbolic communication should not be prerequisites for understanding
the work or reaching a destination.

**Limit:** No game was installed or played. Timing, puzzle feedback, movement,
hover behavior, hints and persistence across game scenes were not verified.

### Bruno Simon

**Observed:** The live entry rendered a car diorama, a visible start invitation
and a sound icon. Two targeted start clicks did not advance this embedded-browser
session. This observation does not establish that the site fails in other
browsers. [Bruno Simon portfolio](https://bruno-simon.com/)

**Documented:** The first-party page text invites driving and finding secrets. It
lists keyboard, touch and gamepad controls; map, braking and respawn; audio and
quality options; and achievements and rewards. These controls were documented in
the inspected page text, not exercised successfully in this session.
[Bruno Simon portfolio](https://bruno-simon.com/)

**Inference / transfer choice:** An optional exploration mode needs a legible
entrance, understandable controls and an obvious way to recover orientation.
Bruno's entry is also a useful scope boundary: require portfolio essentials to be
reachable before learning movement controls. A direct project index should
survive failure or absence of the enhanced experience.

**Limit:** Driving, camera motion, map behavior, recovery, achievements, input
equivalence and access to deeper portfolio content were not directly verified.

### Nicky Case / Nutshell

**Observed:** The homepage visibly combines identity, contact navigation,
categorized project links, images and one-line descriptions. The project index is
readable before entering a work's interactive experience.
[Nicky Case index](https://ncase.me/)

**Observed:** On Nutshell, selecting the introductory explanation expanded
content inline with a source link and Close button. Selecting its recursion link
opened a nested explanation below the initiating text. The original page stayed
open, and the initiating text remained visible above the new detail. Closure and
subsequent focus restoration were not tested. [Nutshell](https://ncase.me/nutshell/)

**Documented:** Nutshell describes contextual expansion, source snippets,
recursive explanations and sentence recap after interruption. Its author also
advises restraint in nesting. [Nutshell](https://ncase.me/nutshell/)

**Inference / transfer choice:** Use intentional selection to reveal useful
detail while retaining its source context. Give the reader control over closure.
A bounded disclosure can serve reading better than an unbounded chain of nested
panels; the acceptable depth remains a portfolio design decision to validate.

**Limit:** This observation establishes the expansion behavior above. It does
not establish keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion or small-screen conformance.

## Bounded transfer recommendations

These are recommendations for the shared plan to accept, refine or reject. They
are not a second set of implementation requirements.

| Experience concern | Proposed choice | Concrete question for later evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rapid scan | Keep identity, project names, short descriptions and essential destinations visible in ordinary document flow. Give optional exploration a legible invitation. | Can a visitor identify relevant work and follow an essential link without discovering a hidden control? |
| Acquire a subject | Intentional selection establishes one subject as the reading target. Retain a recognizable title and source context across the transition. | Can the visitor tell which object or project the revealed content belongs to? |
| Hold a readable frame | Keep acquired text stationary and available until dismissal or another intentional selection. Avoid timed replacement and camera drift during reading. | Can the visitor read, select text and follow links without maintaining hover or chasing content? |
| Return | Make the source recognizable after detail closes; restore appropriate keyboard focus and visible context. | Does closing detail leave the visitor at the source with a usable next action? |
| Spatial continuity | Preserve landmarks and subject identity through changes in view. Stable layout, titles and color can carry continuity when motion is disabled. | Can the visitor recognize the same subject and surrounding context with transitions removed? |
| Reward optional discovery | Reveal useful engineering detail, an explanatory visual or a meaningful connection. Keep essential destinations directly accessible. | Does the reveal add understanding, and can the visitor obtain essential information without performing the discovery action? |
| Recover | Provide a visible route back to the readable index or article when an enhanced view is confusing or unavailable. | Can the visitor recover without restarting the page or understanding a control scheme? |

The comparative references suggest a division of responsibilities: the index
helps visitors choose; a stable selected frame supports reading; optional
exploration supplies additional detail. This is a design inference, not a claim
that these references prove a specific effect on visitor behavior.

## Narrow accessibility source check

The linked W3C Understanding pages explain their associated success criteria;
the pages themselves are informative. The levels below matter when distinguishing
baseline conformance requirements from an additional project design choice.

| Source | Documented requirement and its relevance |
| --- | --- |
| [SC 2.1.1 Keyboard, Level A](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/keyboard.html) | Functionality must be operable through a keyboard interface without precise key timing, subject to the path-dependent-function exception. A spatial presentation does not make selecting a project inherently path dependent. Supply keyboard operation for selecting, reading, following links, closing and returning. |
| [SC 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/content-on-hover-or-focus.html) | Additional content triggered by hover or focus must meet the criterion's dismissible, hoverable and persistent conditions. A preview should remain readable when the pointer moves onto it and should be dismissible under the specified conditions. |
| [SC 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide, Level A](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html) | Nonessential automatic motion lasting more than five seconds alongside other content requires a pause, stop or hide mechanism. Automatic updating alongside other content has its own control requirement without that five-second exception. Holding hover or focus to keep motion paused does not provide a usable pause mechanism. |
| [SC 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions, Level AAA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) | Nonessential interaction-triggered motion can be disabled. The page identifies reduced-motion preferences and explicit controls as approaches. Adopt this as an explicit portfolio design choice even if the declared conformance target is AA; do not misstate it as an AA criterion. |

**Inference / transfer choice:** A held reading frame should work as a durable
state, with equivalent intentional controls for pointer and keyboard. Reduced
motion should preserve information and orientation using stable placement and
labels. These choices need validation in the actual implementation; the reference
review alone does not prove conformance.
