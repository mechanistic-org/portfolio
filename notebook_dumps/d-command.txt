Forensic Analysis: EMI Containment Strategy (D-Command)
Subject: EMI Certification of Plastic Enclosures (Danko Top Covers) Identity Anchor: "The Architect" (Erik Norris) Verdict: Certified (UL/FCC Class A) following multi-stage intervention.
I. THE PHYSICS OF THE PROBLEM
Unlike the steel chassis of the D-Control ("Buckley"), the D-Command ("Danko") utilized injection-molded ABS plastic top covers to reduce cost and weight,. Plastic is transparent to electromagnetic radiation, creating a "Faraday Cage" deficit. To pass strict FCC Class A emissions standards, Norris engineered a composite shielding architecture.
II. THE ANATOMY OF THE SOLUTION
1. The "Skin" (Conductive Coating & Masking)
• The Strategy: Application of a conductive EMI coating (nickel/copper graphite paint) to the interior surfaces of the plastic covers to mimic a metal enclosure.
• The Crisis (Sticky Buttons): Early formulations of the coating were applied too thickly or with poor masking, causing the paint to build up inside the button holes. This caused the plastic buttons to bind and stick,.
