# WebTV Elmer (Zeus) Forensic Report

## I. PROJECT SUMMARY

- **Role:** Lead Mechanical Engineer ("The Architect")
- **Mandate:** Engineer and fabricate the "Zeus" server demonstration units—utilizing the legacy "Elmer" form factor to house "Mercury" architecture—for mission-critical deployment at the Western Cable Show (WCS) and CES 2001.
- **Core Achievement:** Salvaged the "Bastard Box" initiative by stabilizing the thermal architecture and standardizing assembly hardware, delivering 5 functional units on a compressed 9-day fabrication cycle,.

## II. THE ANATOMY OF FAILURE (Heuristic Analysis)

_Discovery Heuristics applied to the Elmer/Zeus chassis evolution._

- **Thermal/Mechanical Crisis:** The "Dead Nuts Flat" Fallacy.
  - **The Failure:** The thermal architecture relied on an aluminum heat spreader bar conducting heat directly into the sheet metal chassis bottom. The existing design featured a perforated ventilation pattern under this bar,.
  - **The Physics:** Sheet metal tolerances preclude a "dead nuts flat" surface contact,. The perforation holes further reduced the thermal mass available for conduction, threatening critical component overheating.
- **Quality/Supply Chain:** The "Hardware Screw-Up."
  - **The Friction:** Previous "Elmer" prototypes suffered from a chaotic mix of metric and SAE hardware, causing assembly line friction and stripping risks,.
- **The Fix:**
  - **Thermal ECO:** Executed a "surgical strike" on the perforation pattern. Removed ventilation holes directly under the heat spreader to maximize conductive mass; increased convective perforation density between the PCB and Power Supply to compensate,.
  - **Hardware Standardization:** Enforced a draconian standardization to 4-40 UNC hardware throughout the entire assembly to eliminate tooling changeovers and assembly errors,.
