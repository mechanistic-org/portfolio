## The Challenge
> **Context:** The companion to the Server. The M500 was a pure playback engine.

It shared the "M-Class" architecture with the M300 and M700.
*   **BD-Live:** This was the first generation to support interactive Blu-ray menus over the network.
*   **Rack Density:** It was designed to stack 10-high in a rack without overheating.

### Project Artifacts
:::note[Specs]
*   **Output:** 1080p
*   **Network:** Gigabit Ethernet
*   **Drive:** Front Loader
