For recruiters and hiring teams: the work I'm looking for, where I can contribute and what would help us decide whether there is a fit.

Product architecture, technical strategy and development methods

The seat

I'm interested in senior individual contributor work with responsibility for product architecture, technical strategy and development methods. I want to work on hard product problems from concept through testing and manufacturing, with enough responsibility to influence the architecture, the interfaces and the decisions that follow.

I stay close to the design and testing. I like understanding the physics, working through the compromises and getting enough of the system represented to test meaningfully. That is where I can contribute most: technical judgment across the product and the way it is developed.

Hard filters

Bay Area onsite or remote. I am local to the Peninsula. Relocation is off the table, everywhere, permanently.
Non-weaponized work only. I build machines that keep people out of harm's way. I do not build weapon systems. This filter is not negotiable and no compensation number moves it.
Real mandate, in writing. Clear technical responsibility, with access to the information, hardware and decisions needed to do the work.

Evidence

Hyphen - Augmented Makeline Kaleidescape - Cinema One Digidesign - C|24 Avegant - Glyph Noon - Room Director

What to send

  1. The role and its actual reporting line.
  2. The mandate: what is stuck, what must change, and by when.
  3. The technical scope, the people I would work with and how architecture decisions are made.
  4. The compensation band, cash versus equity.
  5. The location model, stated plainly.

That gives us something concrete to discuss.
