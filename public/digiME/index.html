Digidesign ME Goes Online!

As the Digidesign mechanical Engineering group has grown and developed our systems and processes have expanded as well. As a result, we have created this site to provide a centralized repository for our departmental information. On this site you will find a wide range of documents ranging from specific design guides to process specifications and CAD startparts, for example. Please browse these pages and make note of what you like as well as anything you think could be improved or added. Your feedback is what will make this site a resource we will all use daily!
