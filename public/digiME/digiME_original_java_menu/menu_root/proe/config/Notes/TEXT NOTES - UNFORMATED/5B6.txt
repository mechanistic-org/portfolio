CONTROL METHODS FOR MAJOR CHARACTERISTIC(S):
A.  PROCESS CAPABILITY INDEX (Cpk).
    1.  MINIMUM ACCEPTABLE PROCESS CAPABILITY
        INDEX (Cpk) FOR CHARACTERISTICS
        IDENTIFIED AS M (MAJOR) SHALL BE THE
        FOLLOWING:
        a.  MAJOR CHARACTERISTICS SHALL
            MAINTAIN A Cpk INDEX EQUAL TO OR
            GREATER THAN 1.33 FOR VARIABLES
            OR A 99.73% PROCESS AVERAGE RATE
            OF ACCEPTANCE FOR ATTRIBUTES.
B.  ALTERNATIVE CONTROL METHODS:
    1.  ONE HUNDRED PERCENT (100%) INSPECTION
        AND/OR TEST OF THE MAJOR
        CHARACTERISTICS.
    2.  AN ALTERNATIVE CONTROL METHOD APPROVED
        BY THE PROCURING OFFICER'S
        REPRESENTATIVE SUCH DESIGN OF
        EXPERIMENT (DOE) AND PROCESS
        CAPABILITY STUDY (PCS).
C.  THE CONTROL METHODS SELECTED FOR ALL
    REMAINING CHARACTERISTICS SHALL BE AT THE
    SUPPLIERS/SUBCONTRACTORS DISCRETION.
    REGARDLESS OF THE CONTROL METHODS USED,
    IT SHALL BE ADEQUATE TO MAINTAIN
    CONFORMANCE TO ENGINEERING DRAWINGS.
