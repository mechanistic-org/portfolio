## The Dawn of the Connected Living Room

Before smart TVs and streaming sticks became ubiquitous, **WebTV** pioneered the concept of putting the Internet on a CRT television using a 56k modem.

This project involved solving unique challenges in bringing digital connectivity to the analog living room.

### The "Black Box" Problem

A major challenge was **RF Interference**. A CRT television generates significant electromagnetic noise, which can disrupt sensitive modem signals.

*   **The Faraday Cage:** We wrapped the entire modem sub-assembly in a stamped steel shield to isolate it from the TV's interference.
*   **Thermal Management:** The device was designed to operate reliably while sitting on top of a warm TV in a passive cooling environment.

> **Engineering Note:** "Reliability testing involved extreme thermal cycling to ensure the device would not crash even under the most demanding living room conditions."

### Impact

WebTV was acquired by Microsoft for $425M, paving the way for the Xbox, Apple TV, and the modern connected home ecosystem. It was a foundational step in the convergence of TV and the Internet.
