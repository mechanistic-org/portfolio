## The Challenge
> **Context:** Ingesting a 5,000 disc collection one by one is slow.

The "Speed Reader" was a bulk loader.
*   **Throughput:** Ripped discs 15x faster than a standard player drive.
*   **Automation:** Could drive an external robotic arm (in theory) or just be fed manually by a dedicated librarian.

### Project Artifacts
:::note[Specs]
*   **Speed:** 15x Read
