## The Challenge

> **Context:** The "K-Class" generation (Pre-Blu-ray).

These units defined the original Kaleidescape experience for DVD.

- **Region Free:** Engineered modified drive firmwares to handle global disc region coding.
- **Upscaling:** One of the first high-end scalers to bring 480p DVD content up to 1080p/i.

### Team Orbit

- **Engineering Lead:** Erik Norris
- **Manufacturing:** Prompt Precision (Soft Tool) -> Steman (Hard Tool)
- **CM:** Sanmina (P/N 520-1066 integration)

### Success Metrics

- **$56.70/unit Savings:** Achieved by transitioning chassis fabrication from US Soft Tooling ($84.62) to Asian Hard Tooling ($27.92).
