# The Sovereign Assembly

I am a Systems Architect and Product Engineer with over two decades of experience in high-fidelity hardware, software, and human experience optimization.

From the early days of **Digidesign** seeking perfect audio fidelity, to the complex mechatronics of **Kaleidescape**, and the rapid-fire innovation at **Noon** and **Hyphen**, my career has been defined by a relentless pursuit of verifiable quality.

This site is not a portfolio. It is a **Sovereign Assembly**—a living, physics-based visualization of the problems I have solved, the friction I have overcome, and the value I have delivered.

### Core Philosophy

**Human Experience Optimization (HXO)** is not just "UX". It is the holistic engineering of every touchpoint—mechanical, digital, and emotional. It is the refusal to accept "good enough" when "exceptional" is physically possible.
