## The Challenge

> **Context:** The "Mini System" (Orpheus) was the first attempt at an all-in-one.

It combined a Server and a Player into a single chassis for smaller yachts and private jets.

- **Portability:** Designed to fit in tighter AV closets where a full rack wasn't an option.

### Forensic Engineering

> **The "Hammered" Lid (Tolerance Failure):**
> During the pilot build, the sheet metal top covers (520-1066-00) arrived 3mm too wide due to a weldment error, crushing the internal drive cage.
>
> - **The Field Fix:** We physically **hammered** the lids into shape with a block of wood to pass EMI testing in the lab.
> - **The Resolution:** Manufacturing fixtures were modified at Sanmina Plant 4 to correct the blanking/forming sequence.
>
> **The "Thumb of God" (Thermal Crisis):**
> The system suffered hard hangs every 50 minutes. Root cause was variable thickness (up to 0.3mm) in the thermal adhesive on the CPU.
>
> - **The Fix:** Abandoned adhesive for **Phase Change Material (PCM)** and redesigned the heatsink retention with heavy spring-loaded clips to guarantee 50psi pressure (reducing delta T by 20°C).

### Success Metrics

- **20°C Thermal Reduction:** Achieved by redesigning the retention mechanism to use PCM instead of adhesive.
- **50% Cost Reduction:** Reduced heatsink BOM cost from $7.12 to $3.61 by validating a new domestic vendor (R-Theta).
