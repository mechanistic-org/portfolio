## Product record

The Digi 003 combined a FireWire audio interface with a tactile Pro Tools control surface. The console version provided eight touch-sensitive motorized faders, rotary controls with position indicators, hardware metering, automation controls, and a jog/shuttle wheel.

## Evidence boundary

The reviewed archive supports limited part and graphics participation, but it does not support attributing the full control-surface architecture, fader system, jog-wheel feel, or chassis design to Erik. This lite record therefore preserves the product context without claiming product-wide ownership.
