## The Challenge
> **Context:** The "K-Class" generation (Pre-Blu-ray).

These units defined the original Kaleidescape experience for DVD.
*   **Region Free:** Engineered modified drive firmwares to handle global disc region coding.
*   **Upscaling:** One of the first high-end scalers to bring 480p DVD content up to 1080p/i.
