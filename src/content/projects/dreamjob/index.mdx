## The North Star Protocol

> **Context:** This is not a placeholder. This is the **Target Coordinate**.

The "Dream Job" is a constructed reality representing the next 15-20 years of professional evolution. It is the convergence of **Mechanical Engineering**, **Software Architecture**, and **Design Systems**.

The objective is to find a role that demands the full spectrum of these capabilities—where "Full Stack" means everything from the kernel to the injection mold.

## Engineering Approach: The Taxonomy

We categorized the visual assets into four distinct domains to cover the full spectrum of engineering deliverables.

### 1. Product Domain

The physical manifestation of the design.

- **Hero:** The "Beauty Shot". High impact, cinematic lighting.
  !\[Hero View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-hero-redux.png)
- **Isometric:** The "God's Eye" view. Perfect 30-degree projection for structural clarity.
  !\[Isometric View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-iso-redux.png)
- **Orthographic:** The "Blueprint". Flat, dimensionally accurate projections.
  !\[Orthographic View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-ortho-redux.png)
- **Detail:** The "Macro". Focusing on texture, material, and finish.
  !\[Detail View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-detail-redux.png)
- **Context:** The "Lifestyle". Showing the object in its intended environment.
  !\[Context View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-context-redux.png)

<Wire />

### 2. Engineering Domain

The process of creation and validation.

- **Prototype:** The "Alpha". Raw materials, 3D prints, and rough machining.
  !\[Prototype View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-prototype-redux.png)
- **Assembly:** The "Build". Showing how the components fit together.
  !\[Assembly View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-assembly-redux.png)
- **Teardown:** The "Autopsy". Analyzing the internal components.
  !\[Teardown View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-teardown-redux.png)
- **Test:** The "Validation". Stress testing, thermal analysis, and failure modes.
  !\[Test View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-test-redux.png)

<Wire />

### 3. Abstract Domain

The theoretical and internal understanding.

- **Schematic:** The "Logic". Wiring diagrams and flowcharts.
  !\[Schematic View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-schematic-redux.png)
- **Exploded:** The "BOM". Visualizing the Bill of Materials in 3D space.
  !\[Exploded View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-exploded-redux.png)
- **Cutaway:** The "X-Ray". Revealing the internal mechanism.
  !\[Cutaway View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-cutaway-redux.png)
- **Diagram:** The "Flow". Explaining the principle of operation.
  !\[Diagram View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-diagram-redux.png)
- **Render:** The "Vision". High-fidelity CGI before physical production.
  !\[Render View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-render-redux.png)

<Wire />

### 4. Digital Domain

The interface and software layer.

- **UI:** The "Interface". Screens, dashboards, and controls.
  !\[UI View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-ui-redux.png)
- **Wireframe:** The "Skeleton". Low-fidelity layout and UX flow.
  !\[Wireframe View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-wireframe-redux.png)
- **Architecture:** The "System". Network topology and software stack.
  !\[Architecture View]\(/assets/dreamjob/dreamjob-arch-redux.png)
