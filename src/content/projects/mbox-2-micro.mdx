## The Challenge
> **Context:** Digidesign (Avid) set the standard for professional digital audio workstations (Pro Tools).

The goal was to create a tactile control surface that provided professional mixers with precise, low-latency control over digital parameters.

## Engineering Approach
We adopted a rigorous engineering methodology to solve these problems.

*   **Integrated Strategy:** Integrated high-resolution motorized faders with custom touch-sensitive capacitive caps.
*   **Designed Strategy:** Designed a rigid, ergonomic chassis that minimized resonance and provided a solid 'console' feel.
*   **Engineered Strategy:** Engineered the internal layout to separate analog audio paths from digital control signals to minimize noise floor.
*   **Implemented Strategy:** Implemented a modular assembly strategy to allow for scalable product variants.

## Impact
The unit became a staple in professional recording studios, bridging the gap between analog workflow and digital flexibility.
