### [SC48 "Lux" / "Capaldi"] Forensic Report

#### I. PROJECT SUMMARY

- **Role:** Lead Mechanical Engineer / Industrial Designer
- **Objective:** Architect an "Embedded Live Sound" console integrating a DSP engine and PC motherboard into a touring-grade chassis under a strict less than $15k COGS cap.
- **Core Achievement:** Solved a critical **75°C** thermal shutdown failure by re-architecting the chassis from 3U to 4U, while simultaneously eliminating expensive aluminum extrusions to meet aggressive cost targets.

#### II. THE CAST (Team & Stakeholders)

- **Neal Breitbarth:** Mechanical Manager (My direct report; resource allocation).
- **Marc Schuman:** Hardware/Thermal Engineering (Partner in thermal matrix testing and sensor data analysis).
- **Stan Cotey:** Product Marketing (Defined the "Profile" aesthetic requirements).
- **Terri Merrell:** Senior Buyer (Managed vendor relations with VTech/Jetcrown).
- **VTech (China):** Contract Manufacturer (CMS) for final integration.
- **Jetcrown:** Plastics Vendor (Tooling and injection molding).

#### III. CRITICAL MECHANICAL INTERVENTIONS (Ranked STAR Stories)

##### 1. The "Meltdown" Mitigation (Thermal Shutdown)

- **The Trigger:** During "Capaldi FOH" environmental chamber testing, the embedded CPU temperature hit **75°C**, triggering an automatic system shutdown.
- **The Tension:** The product is a live sound console; a thermal shutdown during a performance is a catastrophic, "show-stopping" failure mode. The density of the embedded PC motherboard (Kontron 986LCD-M) inside the console created a heat trap.
- **The Intervention:** I executed a "Design of Experiments" thermal matrix, testing chassis heights (3U vs 4U) and fan voltages (5.6V to 12V). I re-architected the chassis to a **4U layout** and implemented a high-velocity cooling solution using **three 80mm fans at 12V**.
- **The Result:** The intervention dropped the internal temperature rise to **22.6°C** (Left side), keeping the CPU well within the safe operating zone and eliminating the shutdown risk, satisfying the "Red Team" engineering review.

##### 2. The PMS Insert Crisis (Tolerance Stack)

- **The Trigger:** During the "Origami Spine" assembly, the press-fit nuts (PMS inserts) began popping out of the top covers due to insufficient material retention in the sheet metal holes.
- **The Tension:** The vendor proposed using glue to secure the inserts. I rejected this as "guesswork." A failure here would mean the console surface could detach in the field.
- **The Intervention:** I rejected the "try harder" approach and recalculated the tolerance stack. I issued a directive to change the diameter hole size to exactly **.170 + 0.00/-0.012**.
- **The Result:** This precise adjustment maximized metal retention for the fastener "bite," securing the inserts mechanically without adhesives. "That's why we don't use glue."

##### 3. The "Blind Mate" Alignment (Exhibition Hall 1)

- **The Trigger:** The console required a "Blind Mate" connection between the PSU/CPU tray and the main chassis, critical for rapid assembly.
- **The Tension:** If the alignment was off by even a millimeter, the connector pins would "smash" during insertion, destroying the unit.
- **The Intervention:** I engineered a "Floating Tray" system using tie plates with **ovalized matching holes**. This allowed for specific X/Y "wiggle room" to accommodate the tolerance stack of the folded steel chassis.
- **The Result:** Enabled a reliable "Blind Mate" insertion sequence that absorbed manufacturing variance, validated during the high-pressure "Exhibition Hall 1" setup.

##### 4. The LED Bleed (Quality Gate)

- **The Trigger:** Light from the "Denko Meters" was bleeding between the LED indicators, making the console look "cheap." The LED collars weren't seating deep enough to block the light.
- **The Intervention:** I directed the vendor to **enlarge the hole of the spacer bar** so the LED collar fit perfectly inside, physically blocking the light path.
- **The Result:** Eliminated the bleed and restored the definitive "Pixel Perfect" look required for the premium market.
