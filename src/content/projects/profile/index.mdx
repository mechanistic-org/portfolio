## The Challenge
> **Context:** In a studio, if Pro Tools crashes, you reboot. In a live concert, you get fired.

The "Venue" (D-Show / Profile) line was Digidesign's entry into live sound reinforcement.
*   **Redundancy:** Everything had to be redundant. Power supplies, computers, snakes.
*   **Ergonomics:** The console needed to be "walkable". An engineer stands for 4 hours, reaching across a 5-foot surface.

## Engineering Approach
*   **Encoder Reliability:** We utilized custom optical encoders with a specific rotational torque (1.5 N-mm) to give the engineer precise feedback without fatigue.
*   **Structural Integrity:** Tour cases are brutal. The chassis was a monocoque steel weldment designed to survive being tipped off a loading dock.
