## Product record

D-Show Profile was a live-sound mixing console in Digidesign's modular VENUE environment. Its control surface provided 24 bankable input channel strips with touch-sensitive faders and assignable encoders, eight output faders, and a mains fader. The wider system supported stage and front-of-house racks, redundant power supplies, and an optional redundant digital snake.
