# Backsplash / Infrastructure Slot

## Project summary

- **Role boundary:** Mechanical-engineering contribution to the Backsplash enclosure and display interface. The August 25, 2021 display PCB dimensional-control drawing is directly attributed to Erik Norris. Electrical design, PCB implementation, controls, software, industrial design, certification, and Makeline-level performance remained cross-functional responsibilities.
- **Mandate:** Package a front-side control and display interface within the Makeline's infrastructure region while supporting food-service access, service, and cleaning intent.
- **Evidence-bounded result:** The archive preserves enclosure specifications, a display PCB control drawing, bridge-board and build imagery, component references, and broader patent context. It does not establish the former system-wide wiring, volume, washdown-certification, sanitation, throughput, or accuracy outcomes.

## Enclosure and interface specification

The Backsplash ID packet specifies a number 4 brushed stainless-steel main body, with the brush direction controlled along vertical or horizontal planes. Its screen concept uses tinted dark-gray polycarbonate at approximately 85% opacity, no bezel, a small candidate radius around 2 mm, and a thickness left unresolved pending the electronics package.

The display intent calls for an NHD 3.12-inch 256x64 module, a polycarbonate light diffuser, and an LED grid behind a lighted bezel. That evidence supports the physical interface concept. It does not establish the legacy Makelight narrative that assigned empty-hopper sensing, operator nudges, or a closed human quality loop to this subsystem.

## Display mechanical control

The `DISPLAY_PCB` dimensional-control drawing is dated August 25, 2021 and identifies Erik Norris as the drafter. It requires deburring and edge control, post-finish dimensions, ASME Y14.5 interpretation, cleanliness, defined untoleranced hole control, and use of the 3D model for complete product definition.

This is the strongest surviving role artifact for Backsplash. It establishes a bounded mechanical-interface contribution without transferring electrical or controls ownership to the drafter.

## Control and build context

The board packet identifies an `EtherCAT RS485 Bridge PCBA 10244R1`, and the Backsplash photo packets preserve the enclosure's internal build context. Those artifacts establish board identity and physical integration evidence. They do not establish the former claims that a particular array controlled more than 70 actuators, removed 99% of discrete wiring, or reduced control volume by 50%.

The patent describes a broader operator-accessible infrastructure slot that can route services, accept cooling modules, and expose windows for cleaning or module replacement. It is system design context and a set of claimed variations, not proof that every described feature appeared in this Backsplash build.

## Environmental and cooling boundary

The candidate 04028VA fan-family datasheet specifies IP69K component protection and three 24 V variants rated at 10.24, 12.36, and 14.48 CFM in free air. It does not show which variant, if any, was installed. The record also does not contain an enclosure IP65 test, an assembled thermal validation, or NSF temperature results, so none is claimed.

Likewise, power-supply and other component datasheets describe candidate parts. Their efficiency and environmental ratings cannot be promoted into achieved subsystem performance without an as-built configuration and test record.

## System-result boundary

Makeline throughput, accuracy, sanitation time, customer deployments, funding, and software architecture belong to the broader system record. The current Backsplash evidence does not make 350 meals per hour, 99% accuracy, a 15-minute sanitation cycle, or TwinCAT scheduling guarantees into subsystem results. The current packet also does not establish a 5.5-year duration or Backsplash-specific production launch, so the candidate remains a 2021 development/prototype record.

## Governance and custody

The shared Hyphen archive is now explicitly partitioned. All 214 NotebookLM sources are locally accounted for through direct matches, captured payloads, or preserved two-candidate dispositions. There are no NotebookLM notes or Studio artifacts in the dated snapshot. Denester, Portion Cup, Makeline, general platform, component-reference, and external research material is excluded or bounded before Backsplash claim use.

## Quantified evidence

- **Specified** an approximately 85%-opaque tinted polycarbonate screen, no bezel, and a 256x64 display with a light-diffuser and LED-grid concept.
- **Released** the display PCB dimensional-control drawing on August 25, 2021.
- **Documented** the labeled `EtherCAT RS485 Bridge PCBA 10244R1` in the project packet.
- **Evaluated** an IP69K fan family whose three documented variants range from 10.24 to 14.48 CFM in free air.
- **Accounted** for all 214 notebook sources without importing adjacent-project or system-level claims.
