## Product record

The KVAULT-10 Modular Disc Vault imported and housed as many as 100 Blu-ray Discs. It connected by USB to an M300 or M500 player, kept each physical disc present for authorization, and left playback to the copy stored on the Kaleidescape server. Multiple vaults could be added, with each vault connected to its own M-Class player.

The product used a third-party carousel with Kaleidescape firmware and other modifications.
