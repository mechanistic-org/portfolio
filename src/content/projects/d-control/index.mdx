### D-Control [Project Buckley] Forensic Report

#### I. PROJECT SUMMARY

- **Role:** Lead Mechanical Engineer / The Architect
- **Mandate:** Architect and deliver "Buckley" (D-Control), the flagship large-format tactile control surface for Pro Tools, establishing the industrial design language for the "Icon" console family while managing a high-complexity modular supply chain.
- **Core Achievement:** Successfully deployed a modular chassis system scalable up to **80 faders**, overcoming significant "out-of-box" manufacturing tolerance failures (The "Swedish Flange") and resolving a critical Adhesion Crisis via Plasma Treatment to set a "new standard for integration."

#### II. THE CAST (Team & Stakeholders)

- **Erik Norris:** Lead Mechanical Engineer / The Architect
- **Neal Breitbarth:** Mechanical Engineering Manager
- **Terri Merrell:** Buyer/Planner (Vendor Relations)
- **Ed Stegall:** Vendor Contact (Mass Precision)
- **Vicky Moreno:** Field Service Manager (Reported "Swedish Flange" issue)

#### III. CRITICAL MECHANICAL INTERVENTIONS (Ranked STAR Stories)

##### 1. The Adhesion Crisis (Plasma Treatment)

- **The Trigger:** During Pilot Validation, cosmetic overlays began delaminating. The vendor blamed "Bad Glue."
- **The Tension:** For a $30,000 flagship console, "peeling stickers" were an unacceptable brand failure. A "Stop Ship" order was imminent.
- **The Intervention:** I designed a DOE (Design of Experiments) testing glues vs. surface prep. Forensic analysis using Dyne Pens revealed low surface energy on the molded chassis.
- **The Result:** Implemented a line-side **Plasma Treatment** station to raise surface energy >50 dynes/cm. The bond became permanent, eliminating the defect.

##### 2. The "Swedish Flange" Failure (Gap Analysis)

- **The Trigger:** Field reports from "Swedish Television" revealed D-Control units were not sitting flush on the stand legs. The horizontal support flange was offset, causing instability.
- **The Tension:** Start-of-production units were looking "janky" in high-profile studios.
- **The Intervention:** Engineered a "Floating Hardware" solution (Part 9420-13080-XX) to absorb tolerance stack-up and enforced strict "Gap Measurement Reports" for incoming stands.
- **The Result:** Restored the "Bank Vault" solidity required for the flagship product.

##### 3. The Supply Chain Crisis (P&G vs. ALPS)

- **The Trigger:** Executive directive to transition from Penny & Giles (P&G) faders to ALPS faders to reduce costs, despite the chassis being designed for P&G.
- **The Friction:** ALPS faders had different geometries, threatening to scrap existing sheet metal inventory.
- **The Intervention:** Designed a retrofit strategy rotating the ALPS fader 180 degrees to align with existing heat sink mounting holes.
- **The Result:** Field-retrofittable solution that preserved 100% of the sheet metal inventory while meeting the cost-reduction mandate.

#### IV. LINKEDIN ARTIFACTS

- [ ] **Architected** the mechanical modularity for a flagship control surface scalable to **80 motorized faders**, securing the "TEC Award" nomination.
- [ ] **Salvaged 8,000+** legacy 68K processor units by implementing a "lowest risk" architecture strategy.
- [ ] **Engineered** a **180-degree fader retrofit** solution to migrate production from P&G to ALPS, preserving 100% of sheet metal inventory.
- [ ] **Resolved** catastrophic "Peeling Overlay" crisis via forensic Dyne Pen analysis and line-side Plasma Treatment implementation.
- [ ] **Instituted** "Gap Measurement Protocols" to eliminate the "Swedish Flange" alignment failure in high-profile installs.
