## Product record

The M500 was an M-Class player in Kaleidescape's networked movie system. It connected to a Kaleidescape server over Ethernet, provided HDMI output, and included a Blu-ray-capable disc tray for importing CDs, DVDs, and Blu-ray Discs. Imported content played from the server and could be accessed throughout the system.
