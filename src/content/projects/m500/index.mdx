## The Challenge

> **Context:** The companion to the Server. The M500 was a pure playback engine.

It shared the "M-Class" architecture with the M300 and M700.

- **BD-Live:** This was the first generation to support interactive Blu-ray menus over the network.
- **Rack Density:** It was designed to stack 10-high in a rack without overheating.

### Forensic Engineering

> **The "Flaming SATA" (Supply Chain Failure):**
> A deployed unit in Singapore failed with burnt wires. Analysis revealed the cable vendor (Pactech) had substituted **Gold Flash** plating for the specified Gold Plating on the connector pins, leading to high resistance and arcing.
>
> - **The Fix:** Immediate purge of non-compliant inventory. We emergency ordered replacement cables from a secondary vendor (YC Cable) and implemented a strict **First Article Inspection** material audit for all interconnects.
