## The Challenge
> **Context:** Before the Mbox, "Pro Tools" meant a $10,000 TDM system in a studio. We wanted to put that power in a backpack for $495.

The Mbox was the hardware key to the Pro Tools kingdom.
*   **USB Power:** Dealing with the noisy, limited power budget of USB 1.1 (500mA) while driving 48V phantom power micro-pres was an analog engineering marvel.
*   **Robustness:** These things lived in tour bus bunks and dorm rooms. They had to be practically indestructible.

## Engineering Approach
I worked on the mechanical design of the Mbox 2, Mini, and Micro.
*   **The Handle:** The Mbox 2's signature handle wasn't just aesthetic; it acted as a roll-cage, protecting the knobs if the unit was dropped face-down.
*   **EMI:** Housing sensitive pre-amps next to high-speed digital logic in a plastic box required a custom stamped sheet metal "coffin" internally to isolate the noise floor.

## Impact
It launched the "Bedroom Producer" revolution.
*   **Legacy:** The design language I established defined the brand for a decade.
