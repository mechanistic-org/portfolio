## The Challenge
> **Context:** The bridge between the bedroom and the pro studio.

The Digi 003 was a FireWire control surface with motorized faders.
*   **Motorized Faders:** We needed 8 touch-sensitive motorized faders that could snap to position instantly without burning out or fighting the user.
*   **Jog Wheel:** The feel of the jog wheel had to be heavy and smooth, mimicking the ballistics of an analog tape machine reel.

## Engineering Approach
*   **VFD Display:** We used a custom 2-line VFD (Vacuum Fluorescent Display) for its high brightness and wide viewing angle, critical for dark studios.
*   **Chassis:** The "Saddle" capability allowed the unit to sit flat or pitch up, requiring a complex dual-injection molded foot architecture.
