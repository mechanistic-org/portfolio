## The Challenge
> **Context:** Before Blue-ray, we had to legally bind the physical DVD to the digital import.

Disc Cartridges were the solution for the KSERVER-5000 generation.
*   **Bulk Loading:** A magazine that held 50 discs, allowing users to bulk-load their collection into the server for verification.

### Project Artifacts
#### Specs

*   **Capacity:** 50 Discs
*   **Material:** Injection Molded ABS
