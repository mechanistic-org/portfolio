Based on the cosmetic inspection specifications, email logs, and visual standards provided in the archives, here are the specific examples of unacceptable paint, texture, and molding defects for the Cinema One:

1. Paint and Finish Defects
   • Spot Rework "Smudges": Attempts to fix small defects by spot-painting resulted in "smudge marks" where the new paint contrasted visibly with the original finish. This was deemed "more noticeable than the original defect" and unacceptable.
   • Texture Fill (Double Coating): Applying a second coat of EMI paint or exterior paint to fix issues resulted in the "filling in" and smoothing over of the intended plastic texture, which was rejected for losing the "sharp" look.
   • Inconsistent Gloss ("Blush"): Defined as a variation in surface gloss, often occurring near molding features. It is strictly "Not acceptable" on Class A (primary) surfaces.

   • Spot Rework Failure: Attempting to "spot paint" these defects failed catastrophically. The touched-up areas created "smudge marks" or "stains" because the new layer of silver paint had a distinct color/contrast difference from the original coat, often making the "reworked spot area... more noticeable than the original defect".
