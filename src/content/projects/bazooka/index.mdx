# Bazooka (Base Station)

## Project summary

- **Role boundary:** Locoroll-side mechanical and reliability lead for the Bazooka interface, with the record tying Erik to PRD edits, reliability responses, tolerance analysis, and production-schedule revisions. Electrical design, HQA, vendor corrective action, and factory execution remained cross-functional responsibilities.
- **Mandate:** Define the wall-box base station that terminates mains power, mechanically retains Sativa and Elvis modules, and provides the physical air-gap disconnect and low-voltage interface.
- **Core result:** The EVT2 record exposed removal difficulty in all 14 dust-test samples, identified a likely slide-lock deformation mechanism, and documented a later passing combined Bazooka/Waldo check. The evidence supports the issue, proposal, and later observation separately; it does not prove that the proposed dimple change caused the pass.

## The anatomy of failure

### Removal crisis: fourteen for fourteen

The reliability matrix records `DUT can't easy remove from Bazooka` for 7/7 Sativa and 7/7 Elvis samples. That is a 14/14 removal-difficulty result for the recorded sample. The prior page called this a mechanical seizure; the source supports difficult removal, not a stronger lockup diagnosis.

The design-issues deck says the large square dimples were likely creating deformation at the slide lock and proposes smaller circular dimples at the same depth. That language is a likely cause and proposed redesign. A later reliability entry says the combined Bazooka/Waldo check passed, but the surviving record does not directly link the pass to the dimple change. The correction therefore preserves chronology without inventing root-cause closure.

### Requirement lineage: 650 W to 850 W

The 2016 PRD baseline specifies 650 W, 100-132 VAC, a 5.5 V / 1 A low-voltage interface, a 105 x 44 x 32 mm envelope, a 10,000-cycle mechanical air-gap switch, and UL/FCC requirements. The May 2017 revision specifies 850 W, but its size field is redacted. The page therefore presents these as two points in the requirement lineage, not as one simultaneous final specification.

The ten-year operating-life requirement remained in the PRD while the action record challenged how it entered the program: the team said it had not been communicated at kickoff, vendors would not guarantee it, and the mechanical design had not been developed around it. This is a documented challenge, not a formal rejection or proof that schedule was preserved.

### Compliance crisis: EMI pretest

The action review records an EVT2 pretest failure, says the noise came from Bazooka, asks the power vendor to resolve it, and requests a certified third-party test. That supports source isolation, escalation, and the verification plan. It does not establish a final passing compliance result in the surviving record.

The mechanical air-gap switch remains a separate safety requirement. The baseline PRD calls for external actuation and 10,000 operating cycles when the control module is removed.

### Finish and tolerance record

The design-issues deck records black-zinc chipping after repeated installation. The tolerance tracker separately governs top and bottom module gaps and identifies open actions. These records show the mechanical reliability work surface without treating every open action as a completed correction.

The former 45.3 C thermal claim has been removed. That measurement belongs to Sativa/Elvis testing, not the Bazooka base station.

### Production schedule, not production result

The archive includes a dated workbook titled `Production schedule for 1,500pcs production`. Its station plan runs across July dates and ends with an `1,800 start shipping` entry. This is planning evidence. It does not prove that the 1,500-piece run started, completed, or shipped, so those outcomes are not claimed.

## Governance and evidence

NotebookLM was used once as a source-pinned retrieval surface and the candidate passages were checked against local extracts. All 204 sources, 21 notes, two audio artifacts, and the semantic content of 21 mind maps are locally accounted for in a dated reconciliation. NotebookLM rejected visual-layout exports for the mind maps, so their layouts are not claimed as preserved. One query-created report is accounted for but is not used as publication authority.

The former public audio briefing is no longer linked. Its generation instructions encouraged maximum attribution to Erik even when the record did not name a designer, making it unsuitable as public evidence.

## Quantified impact

- **Observed** removal difficulty on 14 of 14 dust-test samples.
- **Separated** the likely square-dimple deformation mechanism, proposed circular-dimple change, and later passing check into distinct evidence states.
- **Tracked** the requirement lineage from a 650 W baseline to an 850 W revision without combining redacted and earlier dimensions.
- **Escalated** an EVT2 EMI pretest failure to the power vendor and requested certified third-party verification.
- **Specified** a 10,000-cycle mechanical air-gap disconnect in the 2016 baseline.
- **Scheduled** a 1,500-piece production plan without claiming that the run executed.
