# Xbox: production redesign within an established platform

Mechanistic joined the Xbox program after the console was already in high-volume manufacture. The assignment focused on cost reduction and manufacturability improvements within the constraints of an existing product. I worked on the program in two sprints, returning for the second several months after the first. ^[evidence:EV-79fce1977887] ^[evidence:EV-8639bcd2a02d]

## EMI liners and assembly interference

Working in the production Pro/ENGINEER database, I developed parallel redesign and optimization alternatives. I modified the top and bottom EMI liners, improving shielding integrity and reducing emissions. The April 2002 record documents liner changes, assembly-interference reviews, and files and illustrated markups sent to Microsoft's engineering team and its sheet-metal supplier. ^[evidence:EV-79fce1977887] ^[evidence:EV-936658a94381] ^[evidence:EV-e958b8588c54] ^[evidence:EV-fb5881f12c72] ^[evidence:EV-8639bcd2a02d]

## A second sprint: airflow around the drive support

The later work considered changes to the bottom case and drive support bracket as part of a proposal to replace a power-supply insulator. I modified the bracket to improve airflow and remove a thermal dead spot. The June correspondence records the views while they were still provisional and awaiting design, tooling and safety review. The bracket modifications subsequently went into production. ^[evidence:EV-fb5881f12c72] ^[evidence:EV-8639bcd2a02d]

I did not drive the ECO for those changes. I contributed the design modifications but was less integrated into Microsoft's team during that sprint. ^[evidence:EV-8639bcd2a02d]

## GPU active cooling

I also proposed a fan directing airflow onto the GPU, based on the system's airflow and thermal performance. Microsoft implemented the active cooler. Leslie told me roughly two months later that it was in. I was not closely integrated into the team for this work. ^[evidence:EV-b1d1a9900525]
