## The Challenge

> **Context:** Microsoft wanted to enter the living room.

I worked on the "Duke" controller and the console thermal architecture.

* **Power:** The Pentium III + NVIDIA NV2A put out heat that no console had ever dealt with before.
* **Controller:** The original controller was... large. I worked on the break-away cable safety feature to prevent the console from being pulled off the shelf.
