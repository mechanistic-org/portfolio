# Forensic Intelligence: Room Director (Sativa)

## The Cosmetic Crisis

- **Incident:** 6/7 units failed EVT1 Drop Test due to coating peel.
- **Root Cause:** Serial Number notch in label design created stress concentration.
- **Resolution:** Removed notch, switched to print-on-demand serialization.
- **Outcome:** Yield recovered to 100%.

## Technical Specs

- **Display:** OLED Touch (Corning 2320)
- **Haptics:** Piezo Actuation (Flexure Suspended)
- **Material:** PC SABIC EXL9330
