## The Challenge

> **Context:** The "K-Class" generation (Pre-Blu-ray).

These units defined the original Kaleidescape experience for DVD.

- **Region Free:** Engineered modified drive firmwares to handle global disc region coding.
- **Upscaling:** One of the first high-end scalers to bring 480p DVD content up to 1080p/i.

### Forensic Engineering

> **The "Chewed Edge" (Texture Crisis):**
> The plastic housings (Steman/Weddy) required both conductive EMI plating and cosmetic paint. The double-coating filled the texture, making edges look "chewed."
>
> - **The Fix:** We rejected the process instructions and forced the vendor to EDM the texture directly into the **hard tool steel**, rather than relying on paint texture. This ensured the finish survived the EMI coating process.
