## The Challenge
> **Context:** The M300 was the "Client" to the M500's "Server".

Designed as a compact, silent player for bedrooms or secondary zones.
*   **Silence:** No fans. We used a massive aluminum continuous casting as the chassis and heat sink.
*   **Cost:** Removed the optical drive to hit a lower price point, relying entirely on the network.
