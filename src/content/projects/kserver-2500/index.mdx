## The Challenge
> **Context:** The original behemoths.

The KSERVER-5000 was a 3U monster that stored the initial DVD libraries.
*   **Proprietary OS:** Ran a custom K-OS (based on BSD) tailored for low-latency A/V streaming.
*   **Hot Swappable:** First generation of the "Canister" drive carriers.

#### Specs

*   **Height:** 3U / 5U
