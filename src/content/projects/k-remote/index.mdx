## The Challenge
> **Context:** A cinema system needs a cinema remote.

The K-Remote was designed to be used in the dark.
*   **Backlighting:** Motion-activated EL backlighting that glowed cool blue when picked up.
*   **Tactility:** Custom dome array for the perfect "click" without being loud enough to disturb the movie.
