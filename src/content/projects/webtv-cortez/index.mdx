### [Cortez] Forensic Report

#### I. PROJECT SUMMARY

- **Role:** Product Designer / Surface Development Consultant (Mechanistic)
- **Mandate:** Architect and deliver the reference wireless keyboard for the **Galaxy HomeNet** system. Integration required fitting specific key matrices into a high-aesthetic consumer envelope.
- **Core Achievement:** Delivered a fully surfaced, manufacturing-ready Pro/ENGINEER database in a **6-week sprint**, salvaging the project from ergonomic and signal transmission failure.

#### II. THE ANATOMY OF FAILURE (Heuristic Analysis)

- **Mechanical Crisis (The "Flat" Failure):** Early hard models revealed catastrophic defects: the unit sat unevenly on tabletops, and the top surface was "entirely flat," causing ergonomic strain. More critically, the infrared (IR) transmission angle was insufficient for reliable signal communication with the Galaxy Gateway.
- **Supply Chain Friction (The Matrix War):** A fundamental interoperability conflict existed between Industrial Design (ID) and the vendor (Silitek). ID specified a **19mm key pitch**, while Silitek’s standard tooling utilized a **19.05mm pitch**. This 0.05mm delta created a "growing error" across the keyboard matrix, threatening to crash the keycaps into the bezel.
- **The Fix:** The Architect restructured the entire CAD database.
  1. **Angle Implementation:** Dedicated 28 hours to angling the top surface to hit the 3-15 degree variable IR tilt specification.
  2. **Surface Lofting:** Added organic curvature to grip areas to eliminate the "flat" feel.
  3. **Matrix Arbitration:** Forced a resolution by demanding accurate vendor CAD to validate "keep-out" zones, rectifying the 0.05mm accumulated tolerance error.

#### III. GOVERNANCE & RHYTHM

- **The Pulse:** High-velocity design iteration managed through weekly status reports and bi-weekly invoicing. Reviews triggered immediate CAD restructuring to accommodate shifting matrix dimensions.
- **The Artifacts:** PRD WebTV Cortez Keyboard Specification 2.2; Lite-On/Silitek RFQ; Hard Model Review Log (June 19, 2000).

#### IV. LINKEDIN ARTIFACTS (The Numbers)

- **Billed $24,900** in a single month (June 2000) for accelerated surface development.
- **Rectified a 0.05mm** accumulated tolerance error between ID intent and vendor tooling.
- **Engineered a 3-15 degree** variable IR transmission angle into the primary housing.
- **Accelerated delivery** of a production-ready database within a 42-day window.
- **Secured executive approval** for a manufacturable database by reconciling ID intent with mechanical reality.
