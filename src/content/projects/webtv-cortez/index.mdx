## The Challenge

> **Context:** As Lead Mechanical Engineer (Consultant), I was tasked with designing the reference wireless infrared keyboard for the WebTV Galaxy Home Network. The design required flawless Pro/ENGINEER surfacing for complex, organic grip areas and tight integration of a remote control navigation cluster.

## Engineering Approach

- **Complex Surfacing:** Executed "flawless" Pro/ENGINEER surfacing for organic grip areas, reconciling strict Industrial Design aesthetics (Jeff Jones/PSDA) with tooling constraints.
- **Component Integration:** Merged the "Cambria" remote control navigation cluster into the main housing, resolving complex button height and clearance issues.
- **Vendor Liaison:** Managed technical communication with Silitek (Keyswitch Matrix), successfully resolving a critical 0.05mm key pitch discrepancy that threatened the bezel design.
- **Rapid Prototyping:** Led the fabrication of high-fidelity hard models (Val Kasvin/Sputnik Models) for executive reviews.

## Impact

Delivered final tooling-ready CAD files in just six weeks, enabling successful executive demonstrations to partners like Sony and RCA.
