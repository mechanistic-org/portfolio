### Cortez Keyboard Forensic Report

**Source:** NotebookLM
**Extracted:** May 16, 2024

#### I. PROJECT SUMMARY

- **Role:** Lead Mechanical Engineer / Surface Modeler (Contractor via Mechanistic)
- **Objective:** Engineer the reference wireless infrared keyboard ("Cortez") for the WebTV Galaxy Home Network, translating organic Industrial Design into tooling-ready Pro/ENGINEER surfaces.
- **Core Achievement:** Delivered complex "highly sculpted" surface geometry in under 6 weeks while resolving a critical 0.05mm pitch mismatch between the ID intent and the vendor's (Silitek) standard manufacturing tooling.

#### III. CRITICAL MECHANICAL INTERVENTIONS (Ranked STAR Stories)

#### III. CRITICAL MECHANICAL INTERVENTIONS (Ranked STAR Stories)

##### 1. The 6-Week Miracle (Agile Surfacing)

- **The Trigger:** A hard constraint to go from "Concept Sketch" to "Tooling-Ready CAD" in exactly **6 Weeks** to make the Christmas launch window.
- **The Tension:** The form factor was "Organic" (highly sculpted grips), typically requiring 12+ weeks of iterative surface modeling. The ID team was still refining the "Top Surface Angle."
- **The Intervention:** I executed a "Digital Sculpting" sprint in Pro/ENGINEER, building the master surface model to be parametric. I spent **28 hours** on the "Top Surface" feature alone, ensuring the curvature matched the ID intent while maintaining draft angles for molding.
- **The Result:** Delivered final, tooling-ready databases on time. The resulting parts were "Steel Safe" and required zero major tooling modifications.

##### 2. The Foam Core Consensus (Rapid Prototyping)

- **The Trigger:** The design team couldn't agree on the "Feel" and weight balance of the wireless unit.
- **The Tension:** CAD renderings look pretty but don't convey ergonomics. We risked tooling a device that felt "hollow" or "unbalanced" in the lap.
- **The Intervention:** I physically cut Ren Shape foam models and glued lead weights inside to simulate the target **600g** mass. I integrated dummy buttons to test the "Key Pitch" (19.00mm vs 19.05mm).
- **The Result:** The physical models ended the debate instantly. Executives could "feel" the quality, approving the design for the $30k tooling investment.

##### 3. The Key Pitch Integration (0.05mm Crisis)

- **The Trigger:** Silitek (the manufacturing vendor) utilized a standard key pitch of **19.05mm**, whereas the Industrial Design targeted a tighter **19.00mm** pitch to minimize the footprint.
- **The Tension:** The cumulative error of the 0.05mm difference per key threatened to push the keys into the bezel. Silitek explicitly warned: "The rubber pad on the right-hand side is too close to the edge."
- **The Intervention:** I generated cross-sections to map "keep-out" zones and coordinated a layout redesign. We eliminated the last row of keys (Page Up/Down) from the main matrix and moved functions (TV/Video, View, Codeset) to a separate elastomeric keypad.
- **The Result:** Successfully integrated the standard SK-7510 matrix into the custom ID form factor without requiring a prohibitive investment for new key tooling.
