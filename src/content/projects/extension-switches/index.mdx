## The Challenge
> **Context:** In "3-Way" switching (hallways, stairs), standard wiring is a mess.

The Extension Switch used existing traveler wires not for power, but for **High Voltage Comms**.
*   **Signaling:** We engineered a way to send digital pulses over the 120V traveler lines to sync state instantly with the main Director without relying on radio latency.

### Project Artifacts
:::note[Specs]
*   **Comms:** Powerline Carrier (PLC)
*   **Latency:** &lt;50ms
:::
