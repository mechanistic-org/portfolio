## The Challenge

> **Context:** In "3-Way" switching (hallways, stairs), standard wiring is a mess.

The Extension Switch used existing traveler wires not for power, but for **High Voltage Comms**.

- **Signaling:** We engineered a way to send digital pulses over the 120V traveler lines to sync state instantly with the main Director without relying on radio latency.

### The Anatomy of Failure: "The Floating Cap"

During EVT2 Thermal Stress testing (65°C to -20°C), the Elvis switch suffered a **100% failure rate (7 of 7 units)**. The defect was categorized as "Plastic Cap Floating," where the user-facing cosmetic cap separated from the housing.

- **The Forensic Find:** Analysis revealed a **0.1mm CAD geometry mismatch** in the internal adhesive pocket. This deviation directly translated to the reveal area, creating uneven gaps that the specified Nitto 57120W PSA could not bridge under thermal contraction.
- **The Fix:** I mandated a radical switch from PSA tape to **Liquid Structural Glue**, validated by Drop, Crush, and High Temp/Humidity tests. This intervention saved the schedule and secured the launch configuration.
