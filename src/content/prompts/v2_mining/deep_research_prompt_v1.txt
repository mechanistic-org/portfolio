# Deep Research Prompt Strategy (V1)

# CONTEXT: AGENT_MODE / FORENSIC_MINING

You are acting as a **Principal Forensic Researcher**. Your goal is to find "External War Stories" that validate the internal engineering logs of Erik Norris.
Do not provide generic summaries. Dig for specific timestamps, failure modes, and user quotes.

---

## MODULE 1: The "Specific Failure" Mirror

**Use Case:** When we know exactly what broke internally (e.g., "C24 Faders").

**PROMPT:**
"Act as a Forensic Thread Archaeologist. Search specifically for user reports of '[Project Name] [Specific Internal Failure Mode]' between [Launch Year] and [Launch Year + 3].

- **Target Sources:** Gearspace (formerly Gearslutz), AVSForum, Reddit, specialized enthusiast boards.
- **Extraction Goal:** Find a specific user who described exactly the symptom we fixed (e.g., 'fader fighting', 'noisy fan', 'warped plastic').
- **Output Format:** Direct Quote + User Sentiment + Date."

---

## MODULE 2: The "Market Silence" Diagnostic

**Use Case:** When we suspect the product failed due to apathy (e.g., Room Director).

**PROMPT:**
"Conduct a 'Share of Voice' analysis for [Project Name] in [Launch Year]. Compare forum thread counts and review volumes against its primary competitor [Competitor Name].

- **Hypothesis:** Was this product 'controversial' (high volume, mixed sentiment) or 'ignored' (low volume)?
- **Key Indicator:** Did professional reviewers cover it, or only press release re-posts?"

---

## MODULE 3: The "Price/Value" Friction Test

**Use Case:** For products with high COGS/Price challenges (e.g., D-Command, Cortez).

**PROMPT:**
"Find 'Value for Money' debates regarding [Project Name]. Look for users strictly comparing it to [Competitor/Alternative].

- **Specific keywords:** 'Overpriced', 'Rip-off', 'Bargain', 'Worth it for the X feature'.
- **Extraction Goal:** Isolate the specific feature that users felt justified (or failed to justify) the price tag."

---

## MODULE 4: The "Legacy" Impact Check

**Use Case:** For long-tail validation (e.g., Galaxy, Sundance).

**PROMPT:**
"Search for 'Retro Reviews' or 'Nostalgia' posts about [Project Name] written after [Year 2020].

- **Hypothesis:** Does this device have a 'cult following' today?
- **Extraction Goal:** What ONE feature do modern collectors praise? (e.g., 'The DAC sound', 'The build quality')."

---

## MODULE 5: The "Trophy Case" (Awards & IP)

**Use Case:** To validate high-level recognition and technical novelty.

**PROMPT:**
"Search for [Project Name] in patent databases (Google Patents) and Industry Award archives (TEC Awards, CES Innovations, Red Dot) between [Year] and [Year+2].

- **Queries:** 'Digidesign Patent [Year]', '[Project Name] Award Winner', 'Erik Norris Patent'.
- **Extraction Goal:** List exactly:
  1.  **Patents:** Patent #, Title, and Abstract. (Look for 'Norris' in Inventors list).
  2.  **Awards:** Authority (e.g., TEC), Category, and Year.
  3.  **Reviews:** 'Editor's Choice' or 'Best of Show' mentions."
