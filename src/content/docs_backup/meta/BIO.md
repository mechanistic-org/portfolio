# The Operator

> **Name:** Erik Norris
> **Class:** Hybrid-Engineer (Design/Code/Hardware)
> **Origin:** San Francisco Bay Area

## Narrative
I am a Mechanical Engineer who fell in love with the software that builds the world. Starting in physical product design (Consumer Electronics, Datacenters), I became frustrated with the friction between "Design" and "Manufacturing".

This led me to build tools. First simple scripts, then full pipelines, and now complete software ecosystems. I don't just design the part; I design the machine that makes the part.

## Core Competencies
*   **Mechanical:** CAD (SolidWorks, NX, Plasticity), Prototyping, DFMA.
*   **Software:** TypeScript, Python, React, Three.js, D3.
*   **Systems:** CI/CD for Hardware, Automated Documentation, Asset Pipelines.

## The Philosophy
"Hyper-Functional Brutalism".
Beauty is a byproduct of extreme utility. 
efficiency is the highest aesthetic.
