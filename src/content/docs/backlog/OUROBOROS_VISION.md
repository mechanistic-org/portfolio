# Vision: The Ouroboros Evolution ("Constellation")

**Thesis:** The "Scroll" is a linear constraint imposed by traditional web browsers. The content (Projects, Specs, Timeline) is a high-dimensional graph (The Constellation). "Hyperspace" (V5) successfully visualized the components, but trapped them in `Realms`. "Ouroboros" (V6) dissolves the Realms into a unified, cyclical, navigation-free experience.

> "The scrolling homepage galaxy constellation thing is awesome, and should now impose its structure... kind of thing, rather than being constrained by the Realms." — The Architect

## The Core Conflict
*   **Current State:** User scrolls Down. Realms appear linearly (Work -> Specs -> System -> Archive).
*   **The Problem:** The "Universe" is not linear. Skills relate to Projects which relate to Employers. The "Bubble Graph" (Specs) and the "Sankey" (Archive) are actually different views of the SAME dataset.
*   **The Vision:** A "Constellation Engine" where the user navigates by *association*, not by scrolling.

## Conceptual Prototypes

### 1. The Infinite Canvas (The Map)
Instead of a long page, the site is a WebGL/Canvas surface.
*   **Center:** The "Erik Norris" Core.
*   **Orbit:** Project Clusters.
*   **Far Orbit:** Skills and History.
*   **Navigation:** You don't scroll "down". You Zoom "in".

### 2. The Ouroboros (The Timeline)
Time is circular. The "Archive" feeds the "Work".
*   As you scroll past the bottom (Archive), you seamlessly re-enter the top (System/Work), but perhaps with a different filter or context.
*   A loop that never ends, emphasizing the iterative nature of engineering.

### 3. The HUD OS (Iron Man)
All context moves to a permanent, advanced HUD.
*   The "Page" is just a viewport.
*   The "Content" streams through it.
*   Clicking a skill in the HUD highlights relevant projects in the viewport instantly (filtering).

## Strategic Pivot
*   **From:** "Scrollytelling" (Linear Narrative).
*   **To:** "System Exploration" (Non-linear Graph).

## Phase 6 Candidates
1.  **Unified Graph:** Merge `MultiverseGraph` (Specs) and `ArchiveSankey` (Archive) into a single 3D visualization that persists across the experience.
2.  **Navigationless UI:** Remove the "Navbar". Navigation happens by interacting with the data itself.
3.  **The Living Background:** The background grid isn't just decoration; it's the data structure itself.
