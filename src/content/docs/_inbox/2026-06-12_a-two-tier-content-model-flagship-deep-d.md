A two-tier content model is established for the portfolio: 5-8 flagship deep dives receive "Perfect Page" treatment, while all other projects adhere to a "lite contract" (description, hero, 3-5 hard facts, era/employer context) and are then declared complete. This policy is essential to make the 121-page portfolio finite and achievable, aligning with Epic W6, preventing it from becoming an infinite project.
