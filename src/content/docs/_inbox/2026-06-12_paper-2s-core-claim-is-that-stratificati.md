The central claim for "Paper 2: The Stratification Argument" is that what's being stratified is the *capacity to govern one's own cognitive exposure*, not merely access to AI tools. This distinguishes Sovereign Architects, who configure their cognitive environment, from Managed Operators, whose environment is shaped by external incentive structures.
