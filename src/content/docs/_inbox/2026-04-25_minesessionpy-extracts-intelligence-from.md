The `mine_session.py` script functions as a critical component for extracting intelligence from agent conversation transcripts. It is designed to route identified "Gold" items to various knowledge repositories, including `linkedin_drafts/`, `colophon.md`, ChromaDB, and `law_candidates/`. This establishes a centralized and automated mechanism for knowledge capture and dissemination across the organization.
