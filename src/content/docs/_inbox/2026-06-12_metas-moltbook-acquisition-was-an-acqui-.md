Meta's acquisition of Moltbook, despite the platform's flawed execution and security issues, signaled that agent social infrastructure is a strategic battleground for major platforms. The deal was primarily an acqui-hire, valuing the talent and conceptual IP behind empirically exploring agent-to-agent social dynamics, rather than the product itself. This move prompted every major platform to consider what its own equivalent agent layer looks like, confirming the strategic importance of this emerging domain.
