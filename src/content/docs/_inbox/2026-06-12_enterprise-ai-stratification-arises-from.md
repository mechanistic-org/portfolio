The mechanism of stratification in enterprise AI is rooted in deployment decisions made at the organizational/vendor level, where the value proposition to the buyer (efficiency extraction) routinely conflicts with the value proposition to the worker (productivity enhancement). This commercial logic results in workers in governed environments being subject to systems optimized for external targets, not their cognitive health.
