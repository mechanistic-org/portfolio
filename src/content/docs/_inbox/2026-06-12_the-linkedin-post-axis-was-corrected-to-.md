The LinkedIn visualization for the AI trust gap was initially proposed as a single horizontal positioning axis featuring three markers: 'EN-OS stack' (representing 1000x augmentation, human gates, bounded scope), 'Hassabis says we are here today' (denoting raw model capability), and 'Snake oil claims you can be here today' (implying full autonomy). This was corrected to clarify that EN-OS is built on top of Hassabis's raw model capability, not lagging behind it. The revised visual uses a 'reliability' axis, spanning from 'RAW MODEL' to 'RELIABLE ENOUGH TO SHIP', with markers for 'Hassabis "we are here" (model floor)' and 'EN-OS stack (harness closes the gaps above)'. This reframe clarifies that EN-OS builds on top of raw model capability, providing the necessary scaffolding for reliability.
