The "Limit Switch" philosophy will be decoupled from universal application, allowing the EN-OS stack to support both a "Strict Mode" for deterministic engineering tasks (where failure is binary) and an "Exploratory Mode" for creative drafting, brainstorming, and open exploration. This re-evaluation addresses the previous rigidity that stifled non-deterministic workflows.
