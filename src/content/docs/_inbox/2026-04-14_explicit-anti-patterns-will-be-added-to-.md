To prevent undesirable tones in agent outputs, explicit anti-patterns will be added to the `ERIK_VOICE_PRIMER.md` configuration. These include "No preachy lecturing," "No edgy hooks," and "No incident-report formatting." This decision codifies negative examples directly into the persona definition, providing clear guidance for LLM behavior and ensuring a more grounded and professional communication style.
