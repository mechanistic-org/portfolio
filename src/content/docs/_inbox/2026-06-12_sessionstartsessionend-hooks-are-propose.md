A proposal for 'ritual hooks' involves implementing SessionStart/SessionEnd hooks to automatically run `log_session` and queue the mining process, with an exploratory opt-out. This mechanism is designed to make the ritual's reliability survivable by reducing dependence on manual discipline, which previously led to failures.
