Two core agent skills, `update-resume` and `draft-linkedin-post`, are undergoing significant architectural updates to align with the new tone guidelines. The `update-resume` skill will remove directives for "brutalist, 'Hard Ore' bullets" in favor of standard professional impact formatting. The `draft-linkedin-post` skill will be updated to explicitly avoid lecturing the audience, checking against "edgy hooks" and preachy conclusions.
