A new process improvement mandates a sanity check for NLM report captures to prevent future data integrity issues. The SOP's NLM sub-SOP should be updated to include a step to "verify the captured text is the report body (distinct prose), not the source list, before saving." This ensures that captures are not dominated by irrelevant source-list filenames or recurring chat narratives.
