The `workflow_calibration` context layer has been formally established as the correct designation for mined conversation artifacts that contain process and workflow knowledge. This architectural decision ensures consistent categorization and facilitates accurate retrieval of agent operational insights within ChromaDB. It standardizes how such critical information is stored and accessed.
