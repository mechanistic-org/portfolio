The "SWE/systems literacy" binary for stratification is too coarse and should be replaced by a more granular spectrum: Sovereign Architects, Informed Operators, Delegated Operators, and Passive Recipients. The paper's claim is that most enterprise deployments optimize for the latter two tiers, widening the gap between those who can govern their cognitive environment and those who cannot.
