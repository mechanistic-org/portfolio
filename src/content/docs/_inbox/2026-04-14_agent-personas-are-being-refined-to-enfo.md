A critical architectural decision has been made to refine agent personas. The "Hired Gun" persona will be either deprecated or severely restricted, and the "ME Builder in Public" persona will be explicitly redefined to demand humility, collaboration, and peer-to-peer sharing. This includes adding explicit anti-patterns such as "No preachy lecturing," "No edgy hooks," and "No incident-report formatting" to guide future content generation.
