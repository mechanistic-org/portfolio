Establish `CONCEPTS.md` for code-free vocabulary and `GLOSSARY.md` for disambiguation defaults. This helps prevent agents from conflating overloaded terms (e.g., 'library' as local vs. public) and measurably cuts agent confusion in sessions, especially with internal jargon like 'canon vault' or 'refinery'.
