The ChromaDB and Registry architecture was extended to support 'Context Layers,' enabling differentiation between various types of stored context such as session decisions and artifact rationale. This enhancement addresses a critical gap in the BYOC/EN-OS benchmark and establishes the foundational schema for advanced memory and write-back functionalities within the system.
