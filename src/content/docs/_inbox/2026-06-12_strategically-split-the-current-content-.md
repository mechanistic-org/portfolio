The extensive content, initially envisioned as a single white paper, was restructured into 'Three papers sharing one spine' — 'Paper 1: The Diagnostic' (cultural/historical argument), 'Paper 2: The Stratification Argument' (access/literacy claim), and a third implied paper. This structural decision allows for a more focused and manageable approach to the complex subject matter and addresses the problem of the 'you' shifting register between different arguments.
