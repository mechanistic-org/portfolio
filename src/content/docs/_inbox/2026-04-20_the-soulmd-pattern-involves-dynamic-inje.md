The 'soul.md' concept, representing persistent identity and values, is not maintained as a single canonical source. Instead, it functions as a pattern where relevant identity/values documentation is dynamically injected into sessions. The build intent derived from these sessions is then persisted to ChromaDB. The preferred method for generating this content is an interview format, chosen for its efficacy over a cold write approach.
