Utilize targeted scope questions, such as "you're only touching repo X, right?", as a supplement to reviewing detailed plans for high-cost work. These questions serve as a quick, focused check to confirm the agent's understanding of boundaries and prevent unintended actions, enhancing control in critical operations.
