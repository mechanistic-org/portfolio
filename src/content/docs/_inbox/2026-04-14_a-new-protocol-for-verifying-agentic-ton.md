A new manual verification protocol has been established to confirm the successful implementation of agentic tone changes. This involves generating a test LinkedIn post using the `/draft_linkedin_post` skill after updating the `ERIK_VOICE_PRIMER.md`, and subsequently reviewing the diff of `resume_master.ts` to ensure professional readability and adherence to the new tone guidelines.
