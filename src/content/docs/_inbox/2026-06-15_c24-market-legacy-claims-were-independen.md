Initially, the assistant was corrected on the scope of audio review, having only reviewed `c24-briefing.md` and identifying 34 unreviewed NLM audio overviews. The scope for mining these C|24 audio transcripts was then expanded from primarily corroborating external market/legacy facts to include mining for any new, better, or different material applicable to any section of the C|24 deep-dive page. The extensive review of 38 NLM audio overviews subsequently independently corroborated all external claims in the drafted Market & Legacy section for the C|24, significantly raising confidence in their accuracy.
