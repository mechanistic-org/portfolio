A major architectural decision has been made to refactor the agentic tone from a 'Hired Gun' persona to that of a 'Humble Builder'. This shift aims to transform agent outputs from a 'know-it-all' stance to one of a peer sharing engineering challenges, impacting all public-facing content generation.
