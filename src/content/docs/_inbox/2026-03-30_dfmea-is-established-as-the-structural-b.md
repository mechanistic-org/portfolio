Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (DFMEA) has been positioned as the fundamental "structural basis" for the Product Requirements Document (PRD). This ensures that the PRD is built upon a rigorous analysis of potential failures and their impacts, enhancing the robustness of the design process.
