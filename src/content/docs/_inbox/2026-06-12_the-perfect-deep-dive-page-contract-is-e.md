The C24 project page initially validated the 'Perfect Deep Dive Page' model. Phase 1 focused on finishing this 'golden specimen,' which involved drafting and establishing the 'Perfect Deep Dive Page Contract.' This contract defines six sections of exit criteria (identity, story, structured data, assets/provenance, behavior, and agentic consumption), a validation procedure, and identifies a ready-state cohort of 22 verified pages. As part of this, C24's two forensic reports were refactored into a single authored narrative, its frontmatter made machine-readable, and Phase 1 was completed, shipped as PR #84 (recreated as #85), and validated with zero undefined links, ultimately going live on `main`.
