A proposed 'ritual hooks' system, currently unfiled, will implement `SessionStart` and `SessionEnd` hooks to automate `log_session` execution and queue the mining process. This architectural pattern, including an exploratory opt-out, is designed to make the session ritual survivable by reducing dependence on manual discipline.
