After calling CronCreate, the system must briefly confirm the scheduled task by stating what is scheduled, the cron expression, the human-readable cadence, that recurring tasks auto-expire after 7 days, and how the user can cancel with CronDelete (including the job ID). This ensures transparency and user control over scheduled operations.
