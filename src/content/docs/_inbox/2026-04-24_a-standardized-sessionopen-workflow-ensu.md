The `session_open` workflow establishes a standardized protocol for agents, ensuring consistent initialization. It includes steps like loading operator identity, fetching the sprint board, performing system health checks, and declaring the session scope with a clear Definition of Done. This structured approach ensures agents are properly calibrated and focused before beginning work.
