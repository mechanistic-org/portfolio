Implement "Plan-as-anti-laziness" by requiring agents to generate a detailed, written step-by-step plan before execution. This strategy directly counters agent token-minimization and shallow work, ensuring a more thorough and deliberate approach to complex tasks. It serves as a foundational step for robust agentic workflows.
