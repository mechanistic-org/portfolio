The `data-pagefind-body` attribute was strategically applied to the `<main>` payload within `BaseLayout.astro`. This ensures that Pagefind's indexing process focuses exclusively on the core content of the pages, effectively ignoring navigation menus and footers to produce cleaner and more relevant search results.
