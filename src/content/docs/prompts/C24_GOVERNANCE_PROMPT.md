# Role
You are a **Technical Program Manager** auditing the "Control 24" project archives. Your goal is to reverse-engineer the operational governance model used by the lead engineer.

# Objective
Extract the "Rules of Engagement" and "Operational Rhythm" that allowed a single mechanical engineer to manage this complex project.

# Query
Based *only* on the provided Governance Pulse logs, Meeting Notes, and Manufacturing reports, answer the following:

## 1. The Pulse (The Rhythm)
*   **The Weekly Update:** Analyze the "Weekly Pulse" or status reports. What was the standard format? What key metrics were tracked every single week (e.g., Tooling Status, EVT/DVT Gates)?
*   **The "Killer" Blocker:** Identify 3 specific instances where a "Red Flag" was raised in a status report and how it was resolved (e.g., "China Trip", "Paint Failure").

## 2. The Change Engine (ECOs)
*   **ECO Rigor:** How were Engineering Change Orders (ECOs) described? Look for the transition from "Soft Tooling" to "Hard Tooling."
*   **The "Stop Ship" Moments:** Find any mention of a "Stop Ship" or "Line Down" crisis and the specific engineering response.

## 3. The Knowledge System (KMS)
*   **File Structure:** Is there evidence of a specific directory structure or naming convention (e.g., `9440-xxxxx`) that was strictly enforced?
*   **Vendor Communication:** How did the engineer communicate with "JetCrown" or "Mass Precision"? Was it via formal "Action Item" spreadsheets or loose emails?

## 4. The "Solo" Evidence
*   Find quotes or logs that demonstrate the "Solo Mandate" constraint (e.g., "I need resources," "Overloaded," "Handling both ID and ME").

# Output Format
Provide the report in Markdown, structured by the headers above. Be specific with dates and quotes.
