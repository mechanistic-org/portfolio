## The Challenge

When a page needs a familiar pattern, starting from an existing component helps keep the site consistent.

## Engineering Approach

The [Design System](/design-system/) renders selected Astro components used on the site. Examples such as media headers, figures, and galleries show how those components look alongside the site's type and color tokens.

## Impact

The examples give us a place to inspect how a component change renders before reusing it elsewhere. Changes still need review on the pages that use the component.
