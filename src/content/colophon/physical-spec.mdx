## The Challenge

A resume needs to read clearly on paper, with readable type and sensible page breaks.

## Engineering Approach

The [resume page](/resume/) has its own print stylesheet. It removes site navigation, sets Letter paper margins, and controls where sections can break.

## Impact

The [published resume PDF](/resume/pdf/) gives readers a ready-to-print copy with its own reviewed layout. Other pages need their own print checks.
