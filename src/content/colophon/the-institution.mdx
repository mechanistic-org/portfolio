## The Pivot

> **Context:** In November 2025, `Quantum` (this codebase) was a flexible Astro template. It was fluid, experimental, and fragile. We could change the schema on a whim.

By January 2026, the project had evolved from a "Startup" (Portfolio) to an "Institution" (Forensic Data Engine). This transition was not planned; it was a survival response to the complexity of the data we were ingesting.

## The Antibody Protocol

As we ingested complex forensic history (C24, WebTV, SC48), the fragility of the Startup Model became a liability. Agents—powerful but literal—began breaking the build by inventing their own file formats or placing assets in `src/`.

To survive, the System generated **Antibodies**—rigid Laws encoded in the `GROK_LOG`.

- **Law XIII (Automation):** Fights "Data Drift" by enforcing script-driven updates.
- **Law XIV (Connectivity):** Fights "Isolation" by mandating graph tags.
- **Law XV (The Honda Standard):** Fights "Brittleness" by banning experimental formats (JXL).

## Institutional Architecture

We now operate not as a Creative Agency, but as a **Forensic Archive**. The rigidity is not a constraint; it is the structural integrity required to hold 25 years of engineering history without collapsing under its own weight.
