# Portfolio proof

This context defines how portfolio artifacts communicate Erik's engineering
practice to people making consequential hiring, engagement, or product-leadership
decisions.

## Language

**Primary decision audience**:
Senior engineering, product, or technical-business leaders evaluating Erik for
principal product-development or forward-deployed engineering work.
_Avoid_: Portfolio visitors, AI enthusiasts, agent-infrastructure enthusiasts

**Instrumented practice artifact**:
A portfolio artifact that demonstrates measurable engineering discipline in
operating complex software and agent systems.
_Avoid_: AI-cost showcase, optimization dashboard, vanity-metrics page

**Canonical metrics artifact**:
The detailed static metrics record published as The Pulse and surfaced to the
primary decision audience through How I Work.
_Avoid_: Live dashboard, duplicate metrics page, homepage metrics expansion

**Proof metric**:
A measurement that demonstrates operational throughput and issue flow,
traceability and enforced process, or reproducible system scale and persistence.
_Avoid_: Vanity metric, subscription arbitrage, unsupported benchmark comparison

**Publishable metric**:
A proof metric with a stable identity, precise definition, measurement window,
dated value, source class, refresh state, and independent reproduction receipt.
_Avoid_: Headline number, estimate, unqualified count

**Private metric receipt**:
The non-public evidentiary record containing the exact query or command, raw
output, hashes, and independent reproduction behind a publishable metric.
_Avoid_: Public source dump, methodology summary

**Metric snapshot**:
An immutable, manually released set of publishable metrics sharing one `as_of`
date. A snapshot more than 90 days old is archived rather than presented as
current or live.
_Avoid_: Live telemetry, rolling dashboard

**Atomic metric snapshot**:
A metric snapshot whose required headline groups validate and publish together.
It never omits a failed metric, mixes measurement dates, or partially replaces
the previous approved snapshot.
_Avoid_: Partial refresh, mixed-date snapshot, best-effort publication

**Approved snapshot**:
An atomic metric snapshot whose exact definitions, values, public wording,
`as_of` date, and projection hash Erik has approved in canon.
_Avoid_: Reviewed draft, validated proposal, automatic publication

**Archived snapshot**:
An approved snapshot that remains valid evidence but is more than 90 days old.
_Avoid_: Current snapshot, withdrawn snapshot, stale live data

**Withdrawn snapshot**:
A formerly approved snapshot invalidated by incorrect values or broken
provenance and retained only with a correction record pointing to its replacement.
_Avoid_: Archived snapshot, silently corrected snapshot

**Public operational aggregate**:
A proof metric aggregated over at least 30 days and stripped of private source
identity, confidential work, and person- or session-level activity.
_Avoid_: Daily activity pattern, private-work disclosure, raw operational record

**Metric definition**:
The operator-approved meaning, unit, window, and denominator for one proof metric,
owned by portfolio canon independently of any measured value.
_Avoid_: Display label, query implementation, current value

**Snapshot proposal**:
A manually prepared or machine-proposed candidate metric snapshot that has not
received operator approval and cannot be published.
_Avoid_: Draft dashboard, automatic update, approved snapshot

**Public metric projection**:
The deterministic, privacy-safe subset of approved canon definitions and verified
receipt results that the portfolio repository may render.
_Avoid_: Source of truth, evidence record, raw telemetry export

**Issue flow**:
The 90-day issue cohort measured by created count, cohort closure percentage,
median close time for closed members, and net backlog change.
_Avoid_: Lifetime closure rate, current closed-total ratio

**Change traceability**:
The 90-day relationship between trunk commits and valid issues, with scheduled
maintenance separated before reporting ticket-reference coverage.
_Avoid_: Raw commit count, every-commit claim, delivery velocity

**Durable record coverage**:
The percentage of scoped sessions in a 90-day window that produced at least one
durable decision or finding record, counted once per session.
_Avoid_: Raw note count, transcript count, unscoped session total

**Supporting inventory**:
Audited counts of versioned scripts, executable checks, registered skills, and
supervised services that provide context without serving as headline proof.
_Avoid_: Proof metric, system-quality score, code-volume claim
